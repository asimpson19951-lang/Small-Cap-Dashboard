Skitradesleep Small Cap Dashboard

Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

Skitradesleep operates Small Cap Dashboard ("the Service"), a private, single-operator stock-alerting tool. This policy explains what information the Service collects, how it is used, and — specifically — how mobile phone numbers and SMS consent data are handled.

Mobile information and text messaging

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Mobile phone numbers collected through the SMS opt-in process are used for one purpose only: delivering the stock alerts the subscriber asked for. Numbers are never sold, rented, traded, or released to lead generators, data brokers, advertisers, or any other outside party.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with market activity. A typical trading day produces roughly 5–10 alert messages, and quiet days may produce none. Message and data rates may apply. Neither the Service nor its operator is responsible for charges assessed by your mobile carrier.

What is collected

  • Mobile phone number — supplied by the subscriber at opt-in, used solely to deliver alerts.
  • Consent record — the date, time, and exact wording of the disclosure agreed to, retained as proof of opt-in.
  • Watchlist and alert preferences — the tickers and thresholds chosen by the subscriber.

The Service does not collect payment information, government identifiers, precise location, or contacts. It does not use advertising trackers and does not build profiles for advertising purposes.

How information is used

  • To send the alert messages the subscriber opted in to receive.
  • To honor STOP, UNSTOP, and HELP requests.
  • To maintain records of consent as required by carrier and industry rules.

Service providers

Messages are transmitted through Twilio, a licensed communications provider, acting strictly as a processor on the Service's behalf. Application data is stored with Supabase. These providers may access the data only to the extent required to deliver their service and may not use it for their own marketing.

Retention and deletion

A mobile number is retained only while the subscription is active. Replying STOP ends messaging immediately; the number is then removed from the active send list, and only the minimal consent and opt-out record required by carrier rules is retained. To request deletion of your information, use the contact address below.

Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive a single confirmation, and no further messages will be sent. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact the address below.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored in access-controlled systems. No system is perfectly secure, but the Service holds no payment or identity data, which limits the consequences of any incident.

Children

The Service is not directed to anyone under 18 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes affecting SMS subscribers will be communicated before taking effect, and the effective date above will be revised.
